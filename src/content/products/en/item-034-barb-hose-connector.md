    Barb hose connectors grip soft hose on one end and screw into NPT, G, ZG(R) or metric ports on the other. FerruleX offers SS304/SS316 barbs typically from 1/8"–1" with M5–M24 thread options; hose ID and clamp style set the real pressure limit (catalog notes up to ~3000 PSI depending on hose). Cooling drops, washdown lines and light process hose prefer barbs over ferrule tube when the media runs in elastomer hose. Always match barb OD to hose ID and use a proper clamp. Put hose ID, thread and media on the FerruleX factory RFQ. A barb without a clamp is unfinished — specify ear, worm or crimp clamps with the hose.
