Thread mismatch is a common cause of delayed orders. Our factory can machine **NPT**, **G (BSPP)**, **ZG/R (BSPT)** and **metric** ends — but the RFQ must state which one you need.

## Quick differences

- **NPT** — tapered, common in North American panels.
- **G / BSPP** — parallel, widely used in Europe and many OEM skids.
- **Metric** — M5–M24 style ports on instrumentation hardware.

## What to send for a fast factory quote

1. Tube OD or pipe size  
2. Thread standard on each end  
3. Material (SS304 / SS316)  
4. Quantity and destination  

Mixed threads (metric compression × NPT male) are normal for export OEM work — list both ends clearly so we can confirm stock or machining lead time.
