A **compression fitting** joins metal tubing to a port or to another tube without welding. On instrumentation lines the usual stainless pattern is a **double-ferrule** (front + back ferrule) nut that bites annealed tube when you tighten to the body.

## Why export buyers use them

- No hot work on the panel
- Remakeable joints for maintenance
- Metric and imperial tube OD in one catalog family

FerruleX manufactures [stainless steel compression fittings](/products/compression-fittings/) in SS304 / SS316 / SS316L with NPT, G and metric thread ends.

## Compression vs threaded vs weld

| Need | Typical choice |
|------|----------------|
| Removable instrument tube | Compression (double ferrule) |
| Pipe thread ports only | Threaded adapters / valves |
| Permanent process joint | Weld ends |

## Factory RFQ tip

Send **tube OD**, **thread on the port end**, **material** and **quantity**. Soft annealed tube seals more reliably on gas service — ask if you are unsure about hardness. Browse the [compression category](/products/compression-fittings/) or [request a quote](/contact/).
