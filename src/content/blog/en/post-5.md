Correct installation matters as much as the alloy. Most first-time leaks on **stainless steel compression fittings** come from short insertion, hard tube or skipping the ferrule order — not from the forging itself.

## Steps we recommend

1. **Cut square** and deburr ID/OD so the tube seats fully.  
2. Slide nut, then back ferrule, then front ferrule onto the tube (orientation matters).  
3. Insert tube to the shoulder of the body.  
4. Tighten the nut to the factory prescription for that size (finger-tight plus turns, or gauge if you use one).  
5. Pressure-test before boxing the panel.

## Tube hardness

Use tubing that meets the hardness range for double-ferrule service (often fully annealed stainless). Hard hydraulic tube can refuse a proper bite on gas lines.
