Buyers searching **tube fitting types** usually need a map of stainless options before they open a size chart. Here is the FerruleX factory view.

## Main families

1. **Compression / double ferrule** — instrumentation tube, remakeable.  
2. **Quick-screw / push-in** — faster air and light-fluid installs.  
3. **Push-to-connect** — collet-style tube entry for OEM air lines.  
4. **Threaded adapters & plugs** — NPT / G / metric pipe ports.  
5. **Weld connectors** — permanent joins on process tube or pipe.

## How to choose quickly

| Priority | Start here |
|----------|------------|

| Pipe thread only | Adapters / plugs |
| Permanent run | Weld |
