A **check valve** and a **ball valve** solve different problems on stainless tubing and pipe.

## Different jobs

| Valve | Job |
|-------|-----|
| Ball valve | Manual shutoff / isolate a branch |
| Check valve | Block reverse flow automatically |

A check valve is **not** a substitute for isolation. If maintenance must open the line, keep a ball valve in series.
