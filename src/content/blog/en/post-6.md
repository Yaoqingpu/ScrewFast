A **ferrule fitting** is another name buyers use for a **compression tube fitting** that seals with metal ferrules. In instrumentation stainless, “ferrule” almost always means the **double-ferrule** set: a front ferrule that seals and a back ferrule that grips.

## Double ferrule in plain language

- **Body** — the port or union that holds the seat  
- **Front ferrule** — forms the primary metal-to-metal seal  
- **Back ferrule** — locks the tube against vibration pull-out  
- **Nut** — drives both ferrules as you tighten  

That is why catalogs also say *double ferrule fittings* or *stainless steel compression fittings* for the same family FerruleX stocks.

## When to specify FerruleX compression

Use ferrule fittings on analyzer, gauge and pneumatic tube runs where welding is unwanted.
