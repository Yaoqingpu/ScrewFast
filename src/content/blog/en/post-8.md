**Needle valve vs ball valve** is one of the most common RFQ questions on stainless manifolds.

## Quick rule

- Need **on/off with low pressure drop** — [stainless steel ball valve](/products/ball-valves/)  
- Need **fine metering, sampling or gauge isolation** — [stainless steel needle valve](/products/needle-valves/)  

Do not throttle for long periods on a soft-seat ball valve — that is a needle job.

## Ends and materials

Both families at FerruleX come in compression, female thread or weld-style ends depending on the SKU, in SS304 / SS316 / SS316L. High-pressure series exist in both lines — state design pressure on the RFQ.
