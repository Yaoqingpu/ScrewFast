## Ground rules (apply to every post)

1. One primary keyword per post; once in H1 + first ~40 words.
2. Secondary keywords become H2/H3 sections.
3. Internal links: category page + 2–3 SKU pages per post (see mapping).
4. CTA = "Request a factory quote" (no cart language). Export buyers only.
5. Meta description 145–160 chars, complete sentence.
6. Unique cover per category cluster; avoid competitor brand names in titles
   (Swagelok/Parker may appear in body copy for comparison only).
7. Avoid "near me" / retailer-intent keywords (Home Depot, Lowe's, IndiaMART).
